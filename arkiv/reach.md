# Reach

## Who it's for

- **Independent online shops that want to take USDT.** They get a checkout they add in about 20 lines, payouts straight to their own wallet, and a loyalty programme without building one.
- **Their customers.** Points earned at one PayKit shop are a discount at the next. Small shops can't offer that alone.
- **Developers and AI coding agents.** `npx usepaykit@latest init` sets up the SDK, the agent guide and an MCP server, so adding PayKit to a store is one prompt.

Would they use it? A small shop's loyalty card only works in that shop. A shared, verifiable points network gives each small shop the reach of a chain store's programme, and Arkiv is what lets competitors trust each other's points.

## Try it today

| What | Link |
|---|---|
| Website | https://paykit-landing-one.vercel.app |
| App (installable PWA): wallet with points on Arkiv, shops, merchant sign-up | https://paykit-landing-one.vercel.app/app/ |
| Demo shop 1: earn points | https://kettle-and-leaf-five.vercel.app |
| Demo shop 2: spend them | https://paper-lantern-books.vercel.app |
| SDK | `npm i usepaykit` (https://www.npmjs.com/package/usepaykit) |

Steps:

1. Get test USDT on Whitechain testnet.
2. Buy something at Kettle & Leaf.
3. Open the app's Wallet → Points to see your lot read from Arkiv, with its expiry.
4. Spend the points at Paper Lantern Books.

A shop can sign up under **Merchant** in about two minutes, with one wallet signature, and its rewards appear in the Arkiv marketplace.
